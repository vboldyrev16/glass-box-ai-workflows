# Pricing

One package, one price, one decision.

## The package

**Setup plus training — $2,500 USD, fixed price.**

What that includes:

- Installation of the system on your cloud VM (you provide the VM, Docker Compose handles the rest)
- Slack app setup and OAuth configuration in your workspace
- All eight production-ready workflow templates configured for your data ([see WORKFLOWS.md](./WORKFLOWS.md))
- Up to five read-only data source connections (CRM, billing, analytics warehouse, etc)
- RBAC configured for your team structure: one Owner, Team Owners per team, Managers under each Team Owner
- Live training session with two operators
- Recorded training video the rest of the team can watch
- Written runbook tailored to your install
- **30 days of post-install support** including a private Slack channel, three 30-minute follow-up calls, and bug fixes to the eight templates

What is **not** included in this package:

- Custom workflow templates beyond the eight that ship (priced separately, typically $200 to $500 per template)
- Integration with non-standard data sources without an API
- Hosting (the VM is yours)
- Ongoing managed service or 24/7 support
- A second installation in the same company

See [INSTALL.md](./INSTALL.md) for the full scope of what installation looks like.

## The first three installations are free

**Three free slots, one-time offer.**

The first three teams who commit to an installation get the full bundle, no charge.

Why this is a limited offer:

- I want to validate the install workflow on three different teams before charging
- I want three case studies I can publicly reference (anonymized, with your permission, see [CASE-STUDY.md](./CASE-STUDY.md) for the format)
- I want to find the rough edges in the configuration phase that I cannot find from a single internal install

What I ask in return for a free slot:

- A 30-minute interview after 60 days about what worked and what did not
- Permission to anonymize and publish the install pattern (we agree on the wording before anything goes public)
- A short written endorsement if the install worked for you, no obligation if it did not

That is the entire trade.

## What this is not

To save us both time:

- **Not a SaaS.** No monthly subscription. No per-seat pricing. The system runs on your VM and you own it.
- **Not a free trial.** The free slots are full installations with the full bundle, not a "try it and decide later."
- **Not a consulting agency.** I do these installations myself. The throughput is one to two per month. If you need a fleet of installations across multiple teams or regions, we can talk about a different shape, but the default is one-by-one.
